This kind of display appeared in town and county fairs throughout the US during the 1920s and 1930s to promote eugenicist beliefs, often sponsored by the American Eugenics Society. For a bit more info, see the American Philosophical Society’s [Manuscript Collections Search](https://as.amphilsoc.org/repositories/2/archival_objects/29526) and [“treasures.”](https://www.amphilsoc.org/exhibits/treasures/aes.htm)
