This project began as a critical analysis of [Edward Tufte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Tufte)’s work, but that negative framing was never very satisfying. In the decade+ since I began working on it project in earnest, 

it’s undergone several transformations.

This project has a straightforward question at its heart: What do ‘facts’ look like? The answer, of course, is they don’t look like anything at all — or, put differently, we can make them look like whatever we want.
