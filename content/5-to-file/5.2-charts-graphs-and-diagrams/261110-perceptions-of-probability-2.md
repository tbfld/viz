### Perceptions of probability (2)

For Perceptions of probability (1), see https://www.instagram.com/p/DfiTUJ3xN0J/ 


![[17966597817217555-1.jpg]]


This spare figure comes from Scott Barclay et al., *Handbook for Decision Analysis* (Decisions and Designs Inc.: McLean VA, 1977), p. 68.[^1] The book, commissioned by DARPA’s Cybernetics Technology Office, is a gem from xero-technocracy era, when computational visualization was juuuust teetering on the edge of automation: an old-school typescript text broken up by minimalist figures, which themselves were a paste-up mix of hand-drafted graphics and optically typeset labels.

Props to Daniel Hails[^2] for tracking down this publication. 🏆 And beyond that for his method, which is all too rare in dataviz histories: “I did what should be the obvious thing: I checked.”[^3] 🏆
