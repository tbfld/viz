A kitschy illustration in this planetary/face iconographic tradition by the fabulous Boris Artzybasheff for the 1962-03-02 issue of Time magazine. Digitization thanks to the Army and Navy Club’s Library Trust. Additional things to note: (1) on the publication level, the presence of the annual subscription price and the “REG. U.S. PAT. OFF.”, both subtle testaments to how magazines saw ~branding at the time, and (2) on the image level, the contrast between Artzybasheff’s emphasis on rendering topography — which I think traces back to Richard Edes Harrison, another important illustrator for Henry Luce’s Time-Life-Fortune empire — in contrast his *whatevz* approach to rendering the stars.
