Detroit Geographical Expedition map

This 1971 map, of “where commuters run over black children on the Pointes–Downtown track was compiled by William Bunge and Gwendolyn Warren. It exemplifies their radical project — rejecting geography’s assumption that scale and distance meant BIG, and turning the field toward the micro and the local — as well as Bunge’s aggressively blunt style. When he later turned his attention to scale, it was with a very different focus: *The Nuclear War Atlas* (1988).
