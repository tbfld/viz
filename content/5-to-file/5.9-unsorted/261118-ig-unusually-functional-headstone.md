Unusually functional headstone

FB randomly served this up.[^1] The comments are full of ‘adjacent’ efforts in which the ambivalent function of a headstone — OT1H a memorial silence, OT2H a noisier informational medium — shifts toward the noisy end. I’m still waiting for someone to do an Unknown Pleasures headstone. How’s that relevant? It’s the opposite, more or less: the recycling of an astrophysical graph as a ‘universal’ icon of post-industrial loss.
